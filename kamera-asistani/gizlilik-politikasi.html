The photos you take stay on your phone; they are not uploaded to our servers and are not stored there. When you ask for AI coaching, critique or editing, the camera frame or the photo you selected is sent to the chosen provider’s servers abroad (Google, OpenAI or Anthropic); the provider may keep it for up to 30 days for safety. We do not record audio. The app is for ages 18 and over. You can withdraw AI permission at any time in Profile; new AI processing stops. This policy explains how Camera Assistant processes your personal data. The text in the app and the text on aursal.com are identical.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Ersal Sarıkaya. Contact: esarikaya@aursal.com Write to that address to reach us about anything in this policy, including access, correction, deletion and objection. We answer within 30 days. VERBİS (Turkish controllers’ registry): the app is run by a single developer and falls under the Board’s registration exemption for small controllers (employee-count and balance-sheet thresholds; main activity is not processing special-category data). Should the thresholds be exceeded, we register. Basis: KVKK art. 10 (Turkey), GDPR art. 13 (EU).

What we process

Account: email, display name, optional profile photo — to create your account and sign you in (Firebase). A profile photo you save is stored in Firebase Storage. Age declaration: before sign-in, a birth date is evaluated on the device; it is neither stored nor sent. Only the eligibility result and the policy version are recorded (see Children’s Privacy). Photos: captures, imported images and edited results are saved in the device album; they are not uploaded to us. Camera frames and the photo you select leave the device only when you ask for AI coaching, critique or editing (see AI processing). Usage counters: shots, mode, remaining allowance — to calculate your allowance and prevent abuse. Device and advertising identifiers, app version: security, debugging and ads on the free plan (Firebase, Google Play, AdMob). Crash and freeze reports: stack trace, device model, OS, app version, time of the event — no photos, frames, email or messages (Firebase Crashlytics). Content reports: the text you write, account identifier, app version, date; photos are not attached automatically. We do not record microphone audio: the voice coach only speaks, it does not listen, and the app does not request microphone permission. Ad requests and automatic crash reporting start after an eligible age declaration; this does not guarantee that third-party SDKs on the device process no technical data.

How we use your information

We use your information to: (a) run and improve the app; (b) manage subscriptions and allowances; (c) provide the AI coaching, critique and editing you request; (d) optional usage and performance analytics; (e) communicate important updates.

Purposes and legal bases

AI photo coaching, critique and editing: performance of a contract + your explicit consent for AI processing (separate box). Accounts and authentication: performance of a contract. Subscriptions, quota, abuse prevention: contract and legitimate interest. Analytics: consent (optional) — decline it and measurement never starts. Who we share with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the services that run the product: Google/Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic for the AI processing you request; Google Firebase for authentication, database, optional profile photo and crash reports; Google Play for payments and subscriptions; Google AdMob for ads on the free plan. The device album is not a cloud backup. Selecting AI analysis or editing sends the image to the relevant provider; a saved profile photo is stored separately in Firebase Storage. Provider service terms and privacy policies apply to their own processing.

AI processing

AI coaching, critique and image editing send the camera frame or the selected image and instructions to Google (Gemini), OpenAI or Anthropic. Every request — Gemini analysis, editing and Live, OpenAI and Anthropic — first passes through our Cloud Run server (Belgium). Our server does not store image content; we do not use frames to train models of our own. Providers may keep inputs and outputs for a limited time for safety, abuse prevention and legal obligations: OpenAI API abuse-monitoring logs normally up to 30 days; Anthropic API inputs and outputs are normally deleted within 30 days; Google Vertex AI (Gemini) does not use the data to train models and may keep it for a limited time for abuse monitoring. We have no special zero-retention agreement. Your face may appear in the frames. We do not do face recognition. On-device face detection measures whether a face is present, its angle and expression; it identifies nobody, is matched against no database, and the measurements are not stored. On that basis the processing is not biometric data under KVKK art. 6 or GDPR art. 9. AI permission is optional and can be withdrawn in Profile. Withdrawal stops new requests and cancels ongoing work where possible; it cannot retract data already received by a provider. If you decline, camera and gallery keep working; only the AI features are off.

Your consent is recorded with its version, date and source, linked to your account; you can withdraw it at any time. For the account, payment, advertising and crash-report services (Google) the transfer is necessary to perform the contract with you; the app cannot be provided without them. EU/EEA and UK (GDPR): transfers to the USA are protected by the providers’ data processing agreements, EU standard contractual clauses and, where held, EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification. Other countries: the transfer relies on your informed explicit consent and on the processing agreements in place with the providers. Additional rights under the law of your country are unaffected. For information about transfer safeguards: esarikaya@aursal.com

Camera frames and images sent for editing: not stored on our server; provider periods are in the AI processing section. Photos you take and edit: on your phone only; deleting them is up to you. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The AI providers’ keys are not in the app; they live on our server, and the app authenticates to it with its Firebase identity and Play Integrity/App Check attestation. People blocked by the age check can request access, correction or deletion of existing account data at esarikaya@aursal.com. Buying a paid plan does not remove the age requirement. If we update this policy we change the date and version above. Material changes are announced in the app; if a change needs new permission, we ask for it before the affected AI processing resumes. Continuing to use the camera alone is not consent to new AI processing.
